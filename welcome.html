Natto is a delicious and healthy traditional Japanese fermented soybean dish, deeply respected in Japanese cuisine and especially in Ibaraki Prefecture, its spiritual home.

Visitors are welcome to submit a fictional "membership application" through the form on the home page. After confirming your email via a link we send you, you will receive a satirical acknowledgment letter from the Council. Your email address is the only piece of data we retain, and it is used solely to detect duplicate submissions. We never share it with anyone.

Please enjoy the joke. We hope it makes your morning a little stranger.
